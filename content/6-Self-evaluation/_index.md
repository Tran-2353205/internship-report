>In this section, I would like to share some of my thoughts after **two months** of participating in the **First Cloud AI Journey** internship program. The following feedback is provided with the hope of contributing to the continuous improvement of the program and creating greater value for future participants.

## Overall Evaluation

### 1. Learning and Working Environment

The learning environment was friendly and open, allowing participants to communicate and support each other throughout the project. The online learning materials were organized in a clear roadmap, making it easy to follow the program and stay on schedule. In addition, having more networking or experience-sharing sessions between teams would provide participants with more opportunities to learn from different perspectives and real-world experiences.

### 2. Support from Mentors and the Admin Team

Throughout the program, the mentors were always supportive and willing to answer questions related to both the learning materials and the project. Instead of providing direct solutions, they usually suggested approaches and guided us on how to identify the root causes of problems, encouraging us to solve them independently. The admin team also provided timely announcements, learning materials, and administrative support, ensuring that the learning process went smoothly.

### 3. Relevance to My Major

The program was highly relevant to my major in Information Technology, especially in the areas of Cloud Computing, AWS, and Web Application Development. Through the online learning materials and the **Library Management Website** project, I had the opportunity to apply what I learned in practice, from developing APIs and managing databases to deploying applications on AWS. This experience helped me gain a better understanding of the software development and deployment process.

### 4. Learning and Personal Development

Through this program, I not only became familiar with various AWS services but also had the opportunity to apply my knowledge to a real-world project. Besides improving my technical knowledge of Cloud Computing, I also developed important skills such as self-learning, teamwork, time management, and problem-solving throughout the project.

### 5. Teamwork

Throughout the project, all team members actively communicated and supported one another. During the early stage of development, the system design still had several limitations and was not entirely suitable for the project requirements. However, through regular discussions, the team analyzed the issues, refined the design, and agreed on more appropriate implementation approaches. As a result, the development and integration processes became much smoother, allowing the team to complete the project on schedule.

### 6. Training Program

The program followed a well-structured learning roadmap, with online lessons organized into different topics. This made it easy to follow the learning process and gradually build the required knowledge. The online learning format also allowed me to study at my own pace and revisit the materials whenever necessary. After completing each learning module, I was able to apply the knowledge directly to the project, which reinforced my understanding and improved my practical skills.

## Additional Questions

### What I Appreciated the Most

What I appreciated the most was the opportunity to learn and practice with AWS services through hands-on labs while applying the knowledge I gained to the **Library Management Website** project. During the project, I completed the tasks assigned to me, including developing RESTful APIs using Django, managing data with PostgreSQL, containerizing the application with Docker, and successfully deploying it on AWS infrastructure. These experiences gave me a deeper understanding of the complete application development and deployment process while allowing me to gain valuable practical experience.

### What Could Be Improved

In my opinion, the program could include more experience-sharing sessions from mentors and previous participants so that current learners can gain practical insights from real projects. In addition, organizing more discussions between different teams would encourage participants to exchange experiences, support one another, and further improve teamwork.

### Would I Recommend This Program to Others?

Yes. I would definitely recommend the **First Cloud AI Journey** program to Information Technology students, especially those who are interested in learning Cloud Computing and AWS through a real-world project. It is a valuable opportunity to strengthen technical skills, improve teamwork, and gain practical experience in software development and deployment.

## Suggestions

- Organize more advanced workshops on system architecture and AWS application deployment to provide participants with deeper practical knowledge beyond the core learning materials.
- Increase communication and discussion sessions between mentors and project teams so that participants can share challenges, receive feedback, and learn from practical experiences.
- Offer a wider variety of project topics so that participants can work on different real-world problems, broaden their technical knowledge, and gain more practical experience.
