During my internship from **15/06/2026 to 14/08/2026**, I participated in the **First Cloud AI Journey** program organized by the AWS Study Group. Throughout the **09-week internship**, I studied and practiced the fundamental services of Amazon Web Services (AWS) while applying the knowledge I gained to develop a **Library Management Website** as part of a team project.

In addition to completing the lessons and hands-on labs provided by the program, our team followed the complete software development lifecycle, including requirements gathering, proposal preparation, system analysis, design, implementation, testing, and final product completion.

The work completed each week is summarized as follows:

**Week 1:** [Introduction to the First Cloud AI Journey program, Module 01, and project idea proposal](1.1-week1/)

**Week 2:** [Module 02, project discussion and topic selection, Proposal development](1.2-week2/)

**Week 3:** [Module 03, business analysis, and Software Requirements Specification (SRS) development](1.3-week3/)

**Week 4:** [Module 04 and Use Case Diagram design](1.4-week4/)

**Week 5:** [Module 05 and database design (ERD)](1.5-week5/)

**Week 6:** [Module 06, system architecture design, and API documentation](1.6-week6/)

**Week 7:** [Project initialization, development environment setup, and implementation of core functionalities](1.7-week7/)

**Week 8:** [Feature completion, system integration, and trial deployment on AWS](1.8-week8/)

**Week 9:** [System testing, optimization, documentation completion, project demonstration, and internship summary](1.9-week9/)
